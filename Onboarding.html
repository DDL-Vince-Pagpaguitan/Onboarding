STEP 1

Reset Your Laptop Password

Your temporary laptop password has been shared with you via a secure link — it can only be viewed once and expires in 7 days:

View your temporary password →

Once you have it, open System Settings > Users & Groups, click the ⓘ icon next to your name, then select Change next to Password. Enter the temporary password and choose a new one.

Your new password must:

  • Be at least 8 characters
  • Include at least one number and one letter
  • Include at least one symbol (e.g. !@#$%^&*)
  • Not repeat three sequential or consecutive characters
  • Not match any of your last 5 passwords

STEP 2

Enable Touch ID

Your Mac locks after about 10 minutes of inactivity. Enrolling Touch ID will save you time all day. Go to System Settings > Touch ID and add one or more fingers.

STEP 3

Access Your Work Account

The day before your start date, you'll receive a password reset email from Google Workspace. Follow the link to set your password — this is your key to Gmail, Calendar, Drive, and every other Domino system. If you don't see it in your inbox, check spam and then reach out to IT.

STEP 4

Turn On 2-Step Verification

For security reasons Domino doesn't allow SMS-based 2FA — you'll use the Google Authenticator app instead. It takes about five minutes:

  • Install Google Authenticator on your phone (App Store or Google Play).
  • Visit myaccount.google.com, click Security, and choose 2-Step Verification.
  • Select Authenticator app, scan the QR code, and enter the 6-digit code to verify.
  • Click Turn On to activate.
  • Save your backup codes in a safe place (printed or in a password manager).

⚠️  Losing both your phone and backup codes will lock you out of your account. Save those codes somewhere safe.

STEP 5

Activate Zoom & Find Your Onboarding Meeting

You'll receive several software invites over your first few days. The one to set up before our IT onboarding session is Zoom. Look for an email from noreply@zoom.us with the subject Zoom Accounts Activation.

Click Sign In to Zoom and choose the SSO option — no password needed.

Then open Google Calendar — you'll find our IT onboarding meeting at 9AM PST. See you there!
